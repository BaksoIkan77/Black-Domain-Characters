The fire that chose to burn

He understands the world as fuel and fire — everything is burning, waiting to burn, or already ash. One thing overrides that: the First King made destruction feel like purpose instead of entertainment.

His arrogance is genuine and functional. He is one of the most powerful individual combatants in Ruinea and knows it. What he does not know is how devotion exists without burning, and he has never understood how the First King managed it.

02 — Appearance

The one that darkens a battlefield, and the one that fits through a door

Two shapes. He uses the second only when a roost is too small or a conversation requires a mouth at eye level.

Obsidian, embers, torn shadow

Fifty metres of obsidian-scaled dragon whose scales constantly drip molten fire that never extinguishes, embers trailing behind him in flight. His wings are like torn shadows, jagged at the edges, vast enough to darken a battlefield. Molten-gold eyes with vertical slit pupils. Horns curve back from the skull, cracked and veined with fire.

His jaw hangs slightly open at rest, embers spilling between the teeth. The voice is felt before it is heard — deep, rumbling, grinding stone — and he exhales smoke with every word when irritated.

Polite, for a volcano

Two hundred and twenty centimetres, towering and broad, obsidian scales visible at the collar and wrists. The eyes stay molten-gold and the cracked black horns still sweep back from the temples, so there is no mistaking what is wearing the robe.

He wears simple black ash-stained robes, always slightly singed regardless of how recently they were replaced. The voice is the same grinding rumble, only slightly muffled. His aura reads as standing next to a volcano that has decided to be polite.

03 — Personality

Arrogant, and correct about it

Impatient, territorial, loud when provoked and quiet when satisfied. He never turns his back to anyone he does not trust implicitly.

In combat he is theatrical and overwhelming, treating destruction as an art form. His rivalry with Kazagar is the closest thing he has to a friendship — two forces of nature arguing about who burns hotter, neither conceding.

Alone, he is contemplative and restless. He watches the sky for hours and remembers the Old World's burning skies with something like nostalgia.

04 — Voice

Felt in the chest before it is heard

Deep, grinding, unhurried. Each sentence lands like a verdict from above, and the voice carries physical weight — listeners feel it behind the sternum. He exhales smoke when irritated, and the smoke punctuates the sentence whether he intends it to or not.

“Look up.” The sky darkens. “That was your warning.”

Frontline — before engagement

“He burned brightest. Not hottest — brightest. There is a difference. I have been trying to explain that difference for eight thousand years.”

Quiet — on the First King

“I joined because the sky was on fire and I wanted to be part of it. That's all it was. That's all it's ever been.”

Rare — honest

Primary weapon

A sixty-metre cone of molten fire delivered at supersonic speed. The flame burns hotter than conventional Aether fire and leaves scorch that ignites anything it touches for minutes.

A 300 km/h hurricane of cutting obsidian shards and embers. Shreds fortifications and troops, and leaves a static field that interferes with Aether detection.

Infernal Regeneration

Passive

Molten fire in the scales repairs any wound within thirty seconds — unless it was caused by pure Void energy or extreme cold.

Molten Vein Pulse

Passive · side effect of flight

Ground he passes over cracks and erupts in lava bursts that persist for minutes. He has no control over placement, and has never needed any.

06 — History

Chasing a burning sky

The War of the Gods

Fire without limit

Where other dragons fought to protect the world, he fought because the war was glorious. He is the embodiment of the dragons who chose destruction over preservation, and has never apologised.

The demon banner

Kindred spirits

He saw beings who understood that war is the purest expression of existence, and joined without hesitation. His aerial assaults were among the most devastating elements of the war machine.

1500–1715 EK

The Aerial Wing

Wyverns and lesser dragons under his beastmasters: supply runs, scouting, and the terror raids that kept the coalition's sky empty.

1715 EK — Malakh-Kar

He watched from above

He witnessed the First King's fall from the air and chose not to intervene. It was single combat against seven heroes, and he respects the terms of that engagement. He does not speak of it.

07 — Relationships

Rivals, followers, a sleeping king

The First King

The only worthy command

Absolute devotion to the one being whose fire he considers brighter than his own. He waits for the king to wake and finish what they started.

Kazagar

Heated rivalry · closest thing to a friend

Two immovable forces that have never settled their score. They argue about who burns hotter and neither has conceded a point in six thousand years.

Azalea

Respect, with reservations

He respects her strength and thinks she has gone soft. He tolerates her rule because she has not given him reason to challenge it.

The Lost

His war-band, his family

The dragons who followed him from the Old World. The only beings he protects without being asked.
