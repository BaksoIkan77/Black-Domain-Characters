She understands the world as information asymmetry. Power is not strength — power is the gap between what you know and what they know.

The Revisionist Faction exists because stagnation bored her. Not ideology, not morality: she founded it to introduce variables into a predictable system and watch what happens. Every member believes they serve a cause. Vexia serves her own curiosity.

02 — Appearance

Four faces, one of them hers

The fan is a prop. The shadow is not. Which of the four you meet depends entirely on whether she has decided you are worth the truth.

Absence, wearing a gown

Living purple flames where eyes should be, unblinking, casting a faint violet light on whatever she is looking at. Long dark-purple hair drips with living shadow — the strands move as if underwater even in still air, and the darkness around her hairline is several shades deeper than ambient.

Small delicate bat-like wings fold against her back, dark-purple veined with black. She has no horns, a physiological anomaly among Pure Demons that she has never explained and nobody has asked about twice. Her aura reads as absence: sound dampens within two metres, and peripheral vision loses track of her edges.

The widow with the fan

The wings vanish. The shadow in her hair settles into ordinary dark-purple locks, the flame dims to deep amethyst, and the aura suppresses to social-norm levels. Luxurious violet-and-black silk, lace elbow-gloves, and a folding fan — black on one side, deep violet on the other.

She reads as a wealthy mortal noblewoman with a very good memory for names. She laughs easily and genuinely, touches people's arms when making a point, and bites her lower lip when pretending to think. The room is won within minutes.

Mortal-passingFan: black / violetAura suppressed

Bleeding into the dark

Individual elements overflow into pure shadow. Her hair extends into tendrils of living darkness that move independently; her hands become silhouettes of solidified shadow, fingertips trailing wisps; her eyes become voids with purple flame burning deep inside.

Shadows within fifty metres respond to her will without contact. She maintains full consciousness and full social composure throughout — the smile never changes, which is the part that unsettles people.

Shadow reach 50 mComposure intact

Night, given a shape

A humanoid silhouette of absolute darkness, edges dissolving and reforming, cut from the fabric of night itself. The face is a smooth void with two points of purple flame. Bat-like wings expand to a twelve-metre span, layered shadow that drinks light rather than blocking it.

She generates a field of darkness within one hundred metres where all light dies, sound ceases, and spatial perception collapses for Tier 6 and below. Her aura registers as the dark between stars given form — primordial, patient, ancient. She finds the shape crude, because darkness is most effective when it is invisible.

03 — Personality

Cruel without malice

Insatiably curious, endlessly patient, and bored the way other people are hungry. She collects secrets the way others collect coins.

As Vexia she is cold and slower, each sentence a blade wrapped in velvet. As Lady Violetta she is warm and magnetic, laughing easily, winning rooms within minutes — every question a probe in disguise.

Alone she studies the darkness around her the way a musician studies an instrument. Occasionally the shadows answer.

04 — Voice

Sibilant, and never raised

Sibilant, whispery, smooth as silk, and never raised. As Vexia the words land like blades wrapped in velvet, each sentence deliberate and slow. As Lady Violetta the tone turns warm and playful, and every question is a probe wearing a compliment.

“Your plan has four exits. I have occupied three.” The shadows at her feet deepen. “Choose the fourth while it exists.”

Vexia — assessing

“Boredom is the only sin I cannot forgive in myself. The faction is an antidote.” Silent laughter. “You already knew. The question is what you plan to do with it — and I find that question delightful.”

Vexia — to Alleen

“You are the only thing in this nation I have not figured out. Do not mistake my patience for indifference — I have waited three thousand years, and I can wait three more.”

06 — History

An invisible architect

≈ 28,000 BR

Born to the dark

Shadows responded to her before she had words, and the dark around her cradle moved when she dreamed. Other demons sharpened claws; she lingered in corners.

The War of the Gods

The web before the battle

She disassembled enemy command structures from within before the armies ever met. No one knew her name; everyone felt her touch.

≈ 12,000–0 BR — The Breaking

Darkness in the cracks

She watched shadow pool between collapsing dimensions and understood something new: shadow is not the absence of light but the space between things — the gaps where truth hides.

1715 EK — after

Silkhaven and the Revisionists

Azalea offered a seat after a year of silent observation. Vexia accepted because the offer was interesting. The faction came later, founded to cure a boredom no one else could see.

07 — Relationships

Everyone is a piece, except one

Azalea

The only unread page

Loyal only while Azalea remains fascinating. She suspects the Unraveler hides something fundamental and pursues the thread for pure intrigue.

Alleen

Mutual transparent awareness

Alleen knows about the faction. Vexia knows Alleen knows. Neither has acted. Polite chess where both see the whole board.

Nuva

The one blind spot

Nuva's dimensional offset disrupts shadow perception. Vexia cannot track her the way she tracks everyone else, and it unsettles her badly.

Vorlag

Avoided entirely

His Immutable Ward makes him immune to every tool in her portfolio. The one shadow that does not move when she commands it.

08 — Truths

The web with no edge

Open the sealed record
Truth

The faction is boredom wearing a cause

She founded it to introduce chaos into a predictable system and observe. She has never believed in it for a single hour.

Fracture

She has lost track of her own network

Sections of the web she has been expanding for millennia are no longer mapped. She is not certain what lives in the deepest parts.

Contradiction

The most informed being knows least about herself

Twenty-eight thousand years cataloguing everyone else's secrets, and never once her own. The day she does, she will find that curiosity is not a personality trait — it is the only thing keeping her from becoming what she hunts.
