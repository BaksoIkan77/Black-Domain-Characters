01 — Overview

The hammer that never learned to be a scalpel

He understands the world through one metric: strength — not as dominance, but as the capacity to protect what matters and break what threatens it.

His simplicity is a framework, not a limitation. He tracks every battle, every opponent, every scar in perfect detail. He simply has no interest in problems that cannot be solved with force, because he has never met one that survived the application.

02 — Appearance

Living magma

One form. He has never used another, and considers the idea dishonest — a humanoid disguise would register to him as a lie, and he does not lie.

Four and a half metres of cracked obsidian plates over a molten core, orange-red glowing between the fissures. Thick blunt horns jut from a broad skull, the fists are the size of boulders, and every step scorches earth and leaves a smouldering trail. No wings, no tail, no adornment.

The glow is a readout rather than decoration: bright orange-red is rage, deep ember is calm, near-dark is grief. He radiates constant heat — ten metres of discomfort, three metres of physical pain for unprotected flesh. His aura reads as an elemental disaster zone rather than an individual.

03 — Personality

Loud, honest, and warmer than he looks

He has never used Elite Form. A humanoid disguise would register to him as a lie, and he does not lie.

On the frontier he is relentless and joyful, charging first and planning after, treating battle as a celebration. In private he is generous with allies, fiercely protective of Gorath, and quick to laugh.

In the rare quiet moments he is reflective in ways nobody expects. He sits with Gorath in comfortable silence and watches fire the way other people watch water.

04 — Voice

Each word punctuated by steam

Booming, each word punctuated by a burst of steam from his jaw. Short, rhetorical, and he never waits for an answer. Volume is constant — quiet is not in his register.

“COME ON! ALL OF YOU! I HAVE NOT HIT ANYTHING IN THREE DAYS!”

Frontline — before a charge

“He hit gods. Actual gods. And he kept walking.” Pause. “I will keep walking until he gets up.”

Quieter — on the First King

“She's strong. Smarter than me — everyone is smarter than me. But strong. That's enough.” A fist cracks the table. “For now, that's enough.”

Honest — on Azalea

Heat Aura

Passive · permanent

Ten metres of discomfort and equipment degradation, three metres of physical pain. Metal softens, stone cracks, water evaporates. He cannot suppress it, even in sleep.

Lava Generation

Active

Molten projectiles from fist-sized to boulder-sized, launched at will. Used for area denial and siege breaking.

LimitAccuracy drops beyond 100 m.

Earth-Shaking Stomp

Active · 2s windup

A kinetic shockwave that topples walls and cracks ground up to thirty metres. Everything standing goes down; everything built comes apart.

Molten Core Regeneration

Passive

Obsidian plates self-repair through molten flow. Physical damage heals as long as the core burns — cold-based magic is the only thing that slows it.

06 — History

Forged for war, and never left it

≈ 55,000 BR

One of the first

Created directly by the Goddess of Abyss alongside the First King, Azalea, and Gorath. Forged for war and found in it the only honest language.

The War of the Gods

Forty thousand years

He fought beside the First King from the first campaign to the last. When the Goddess fell and the command structure fractured, he stayed without needing to think about it.

1500–1715 EK

The southern assault

A blunt instrument driving through allied lines with Gorath at his side — the single most destructive element of the demon offensive.

1715 EK — Malakh-Kar

Three hours late

He was at the southern front when the First King fell. The news arrived three hours after the fact. Those hours sit in him as unspent heat.

07 — Relationships

The only soft thing about him

Gorath

Battle brother

The only being he calls friend without flinching. They fight as one front and have never been separated. His warmth toward Gorath is the only soft thing about him.

The First King

Absolute loyalty

The strongest thing he has ever seen. He waits for the First King to wake with the patience of a forge — constant, hot, ready.

Azalea

Respect based on strength

He follows because she is strong. He has never questioned her authority because she has never given him reason to.

Zar'goth

Heated rivalry

Two immovable forces that have never settled their score. Their arguments are seismic events and neither has conceded.

08 — Truths

What the glow hides

Truth

The ledger is perfect

Every kill, every wound, every fallen ally, sixty millennia deep. His simplicity is a framework that carries more memory than most scholars.

Fracture

Three hours

The delay between the First King's fall and the news reaching the southern front. He has never spoken of what he would have done differently.

Contradiction

He mourns every kill

The most destructive force on the southern frontier is the one who grieves each death. The glow dims after every battle, and nobody asks why.
