The blade that outlived its wielder

He rose from the mingled blood of two goddesses before anything in Ruinea had a name. He follows one being by choice, and that being has been asleep for nine hundred years.

Vorlag treats the throne as a regency. He assessed Azalea, found her strong, and permitted the coronation — the word is accurate in his mind. He has stated within the command structure that if her strength falters, he will remove her. By force if necessary. He says this calmly, the way one states a weather forecast.

02 — Appearance

Two shapes, one announcement

He removes the coat before either form becomes dangerous. The gesture is the warning, and he has never needed armour.

The gaunt aristocrat

Two hundred and ten centimetres of ash-white elegance over angular bone — silver-white hair fine as silk falling past the shoulders, blood-moon eyes set deep, fangs visible whenever he speaks at length. Clawed fingertips, dark and polished. A small tail worn openly, because in the Black Domain a vampire's nature is a statement rather than a secret.

The coat is archaic black with a high collar and no ornament at all. His aura carries ancient predatory weight: anything at Tier 5 or below within ten metres experiences involuntary submission, and any vampire reads his First Blood heritage as a pressure older than memory.

What the coat was hiding

Limbs elongate past two hundred and fifty centimetres and the fingers split into razor-obsidian claws. An eight-metre span of leathery wing unfurls from his back, the eyes swell to solid crimson, and the skin tightens over a lengthened skeleton. The tail thickens into a whip. His voice drops into a low vibration that originates in the chest rather than the throat.

Within thirty metres the darkness itself bends toward him and light dims passively. He does not project fear in this shape — he becomes the environment that fear is an appropriate response to.

03 — Personality

Patience as a blade

He would never describe himself as lonely. He would describe himself as undeployed.

Publicly he is diplomatic and measured, coldly polite, honest in a way that lands as cruelty. On the frontier he is absolute and economical — he walks to the front position before a battle and transforms without announcement, because the change itself is the signal.

Privately he faces east after dark, stands at the highest point available, and speaks to no one. At dawn he returns to function as though the vigil never happened. He takes no orders after dark because night is the only time he allows himself to be fully what he is.

04 — Voice

Whispered from deep in the chest

Whispered, slow, echoing — the voice travels up from the chest rather than the throat. Long pauses between sentences. He never repeats himself, and addresses most beings as though their death is scheduled and merely unconfirmed.

“Hold the seam. Reinforce the third pass. I will be there before the moon reaches the ridge.” Silence. “Do not make me arrive twice.”

Frontier — to war-band commanders

“He stood against gods. Not because he believed he would win. Because the alternative was standing nowhere.” Long silence. “I was three days' march away. Three days.”

Rare — on the First King

“I follow strength. It is the only honest currency. When hers fades, I will know before she does.”

Private — on Azalea

06 — History

Older than the word for age

Before the Age of Discord

First Blood

One of a handful of vampires risen from the mingled blood of Beginning and Ending. He predates nearly every civilization in existence and remembers a world without cycles.

The War of the Gods

The pledge

When the Goddess of Abyss fell and her forces lost their anchor, the First King kept advancing without pause. Vorlag recognised a will that matched the scale of the conflict, and pledged himself to it.

1500–1715 EK

The vanguard

He commanded the forward element that broke enemy lines before the main body engaged. Two centuries, one unbroken record, one regret.

1715 EK — Malakh-Kar

Three days' march

He held the northern front while the First King faced seven heroes. By the time the news reached him, the war was over. He has never forgiven the distance.

After

The regency

He permitted Azalea's coronation, requested the most exposed post in the nation, and has been caching relics ever since for the moment his master wakes.

07 — Relationships

Where his loyalty points

The First King

The only choice he ever made

Absolute devotion to the sleeping ruler beneath Malakh-Kar. Personal, not doctrinal — he follows the man, not the mandate. He has spent centuries caching knowledge and relics across the Domain.

Azalea

Conditional respect

He acknowledges her power and tolerates her rule. Neither has tested the other. The mutual calculation is maintained at council-table distance and neither blinks first.

Cassia the Veil

Deceased · ancient enmity

They clashed millennia ago over the First King's relics. He has never forgiven the insult, and does not know she faded into the Mist. He still holds the grudge.

Noctisia's vampires

Deep, specific contempt

They chose discipline and hiding. He chose strength and war. He regards them as a race that abandoned its own nature.

08 — Truths

What he never explains

Open the sealed record
Truth

The throne is a regency

Azalea sits on it because the First King sleeps. Vorlag treats her rule as temporary by definition and will redirect his loyalty the moment his master opens his eyes.

Fracture

Three days

He has positioned himself within one day's march of every strategic point since Malakh-Kar, and has never explained why.

Contradiction

Diplomat and body count

He is the Domain's diplomatic face — patient, measured, terrifying without violence — and the being who has personally killed more enemy commanders than any other living entity. He sees no tension between the two.
