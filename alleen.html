She processes the world as a spatial equation. Every entity occupies coordinates, every conflict resolves through positioning, every outcome is a function of distance, timing, and force vectors.

She holds the road nexus through which every supply line in the Black Domain runs. She has never used that as leverage. She has never needed to.

02 — Appearance

Three states, dialled upward

She shifts in stages — hair, then hands, then eyes — as though turning a dial. She finds the third state excessive, and uses it anyway when survival demands.

Regal, and entirely unscathed

Tall and regal with an ideal hourglass build, flawless pale skin over sharp aristocratic bone. Amethyst eyes that rarely blink and never hurry. Long dark blue hair, straight and perfectly kept, falling past the shoulders.

Two elegantly curved horns arc from her temples, polished and untouched — she has never let them take a blow. Wings and tail stay retracted. Her hand rests on her sword pommel as a habit of authority, and her aura reads as controlled cosmic pressure: something vast, held behind glass.

Starlight in stages

Hair dissolves first into drifting starlight and nebula dust. Then the hands become deep-space darkness with points of light orbiting the fingers. Then the eyes shift from amethyst to raw stellar white, the pupils collapsing into miniature galaxies.

Her aura expands to twenty metres of mild spatial distortion — gravity fluctuates, compasses spin, and distance perception warps for anyone standing inside it. She keeps full consciousness and full control throughout. Each partial element grants proportional access to cosmic-scale output.

A walking starfield

Three hundred centimetres of humanoid silhouette composed entirely of deep space — galaxies and nebulae drifting across the surface, black between the stars. The face becomes a void with two points of concentrated stellar light. Her horns extend into curved spirals of compressed cosmic matter, and her limbs trail stardust that persists in the air for minutes.

Gravitational distortion reaches two hundred metres: unanchored objects drift toward her, light bends, and spatial perception collapses entirely for Tier 6 and below. Her aura registers as a celestial event rather than a presence.

03 — Personality

A smile used so long it became furniture

Terrifyingly patient, philosophical, quietly certain. She never raises her voice and never wastes a word.

Every conversation is a chess move, delivered warmly. The smile never reaches her eyes — she has used it as tool, mask, and habit for so long that she no longer remembers which it started as.

Alone, she is wearier and softer. She watches the stars for hours without purpose and traces constellations on tabletops with one finger.

04 — Voice

Every sentence lands like a verdict

Soft, measured, hypnotic. Every sentence lands like a verdict. Long intentional pauses before she answers, and she never raises her volume — not even when killing. She never repeats herself.

“You order the withdrawal, and the radicals call it weakness. You hold the advance, and the Theocracy calls it aggression. The geometry of this position has no clean solution.” A pause. “It never did.”

Private — to Azalea

“Space is not empty. It is the fabric everything else is standing on. When you understand that, you stop throwing fire and start folding.”

On her discipline

“I watched the sky come apart. Not metaphorically. The sky — the space between stars — cracked like glass. And inside the cracks, I saw the geometry.”

Rare — on the Breaking

06 — History

Reading the architecture of a dying world

≈ 25,000 BR

A prodigy of the Weave

Born with an innate affinity for space and the void — rare among demons. She perceived the geometry of reality before she had a name.

The War of the Gods

The one who saw the board

Positioned armies through spatial folding and collapsed formations through dimensional compression. Among the commanders, she was the one who saw the entire board.

≈ 12,000–0 BR — The Breaking

The sky cracked

She survived because she could fold space around herself faster than it could collapse. Where others saw apocalypse, she saw structure failing — and read the geometry in real time.

1715 EK

The layered nation

She supported Azalea's claim, then built the satellite system, the supply corridors, and the closed economic units that keep the Domain functional. Spatial logistics no other nation can replicate.

07 — Relationships

Where her geometry points

Closest philosophical ally

Shared doctrine of order over destruction. She suspects Azalea conceals something fundamental and finds the question fascinating rather than threatening.

Vexia

Knowing, unused leverage

She knows Vexia founded the Revisionist Faction and has said nothing. Their interactions are polite chess games where both players see the whole board.

Vorlag

A fixed point

His spatial immutability registers on her perception as something her folding cannot affect. Unusual enough to command attention.

Nuva

Spatially fascinating, professionally irritating

She frequently asks where Azalea disappears to. Nuva answers nothing, and Alleen finds the silence structurally interesting.

08 — Truths

What holds the sky together

Truth

Her mastery was learned from a corpse

Every spell she casts is built on the memory of reality dying. She did not study cosmic Weave; she read it off a collapsing dimension.

Fracture

She has never gone full cosmic in the New World

The last complete manifestation was during the Breaking. Being a walking starfield while everything ended left a mark she addresses through restraint.

Contradiction

The most ordered mind carries the most chaotic knowledge

Her control is the discipline of someone who knows exactly how fragile the fabric is, holding it together through sheer refusal to let it slip again.
