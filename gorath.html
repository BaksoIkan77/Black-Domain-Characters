The mountain that walks

He sorts the world into things to protect and things to break. The First King is the first category. Everything else sorts itself.

His loyalty is not philosophical. It is geological. The First King was the first thing that made standing still feel worth something, and he has been standing at that side ever since.

02 — Appearance

A body that is a record

One form, and he has never used another. The stone, the scars, and the notches are his history — hiding it would be erasing it.

Six metres built like a mountain given legs. Deep grey-brown stone cracked over orange fissures that never fully cool. Massive horns curve forward from the skull, each carved with a notch after every kill — hundreds of them, the oldest worn smooth, the newest still sharp. His knuckles are permanently scarred from battering fortress walls.

His stillness reads as terrain. Detection magic and threat assessment register him as a landscape feature until he moves, and both allies and enemies are visibly surprised when the mountain walks.

03 — Personality

Economical in everything

Single words when possible. Short sentences when necessary. He has never asked a question in his life.

With Kazagar he is warm in grunts — shoulder-checks, shared fire, occasional almost-humour. He is the only one who makes Gorath speak in sentences.

Alone he is still and watchful, running his thumb along the notches oldest to newest, like reading a ledger. Sometimes he counts under his breath.

04 — Voice

Stone grinding on stone

Low, rumbling, few words. Single words when possible, short sentences when necessary. He never asks a question, never debates, never repeats himself — and the voice sounds like stone grinding against stone.

“Break.” Steps forward. The ground shifts.

Frontline — before engagement

“First King says fight. I fight.” Silence. “First King says stop. I stop.”

On loyalty

“Three hours. We were three hours away.” The fissures dim. “That's a lot of ground.”

Rare — on Malakh-Kar

The anvil

Signature capabilities

Stone-Heart Resilience

Passive

Eighty percent reduction against all physical attacks. Cracked-stone hide absorbs impacts that shatter lesser demons outright.

LimitMagical attacks at Circle 6+ bypass the reduction entirely.

Earth-Rend Stomp

Active · 1s windup

A seismic shockwave up to forty metres. Topples structures, knocks foes prone, splits ground into temporary chasms.

Crackfire Burst

Triggered by genuine rage

Skin fissures erupt, spewing molten arcs in a fifteen-metre radius. Post-burst fatigue for twenty minutes. He decides whether to let the anger in — and the one time it happens, even Kazagar steps back.

Kill-Notch Binding

Passive · permanent

Each notch links his life force to a fallen foe, granting a fraction of accumulated strength. Minuscule per notch, significant over sixty millennia.

06 — History

Fifty-eight thousand years of standing still

≈ 58,000 BR

Made to break things

Created directly by the Goddess of Abyss. He found in the breaking a kind of purpose that suited him and never questioned it again.

The War of the Gods

The longest presence

Forty thousand years at the First King's side — the most consistent presence of any living being. When the Goddess fell, he did not change direction.

1715 EK — Malakh-Kar

847 notches since

He was at the northern front. The news arrived three hours late. He has carved eight hundred and forty-seven notches since that day — more than the previous fifty thousand years combined.

After

Stonefall

He waited for Kazagar's verdict on the new sovereign. Kazagar said she's strong. Gorath accepted, and has obeyed the Unraveler as a steward ever since.

07 — Relationships

Two things he protects

Kazagar

Battle brother

The only living being he protects as fiercely as the First King. Sixty millennia without a single separation. He is the only one who makes Gorath speak in sentences.

The First King

Foundational loyalty

The reason he stands anywhere at all. He waits with geological patience for the king to wake.

Azalea

Obedient as steward

She is not the First King. He obeys because the First King has not commanded otherwise.

The war-bands

Trusted by presence

They trust him because he never moves backward. His presence at Stonefall is the reason the southern front holds.
