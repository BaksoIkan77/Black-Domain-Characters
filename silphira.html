The flame moths cannot resist

She does not view cruelty as cruelty. Disassembly is research, and desire is her sharpest tool — fear builds walls, but desire makes people open the door themselves.

Born monstrous and instinct-heavy, she catalogued demon behaviour for centuries until no variation remained. Then humans broke her framework: afraid and advancing anyway, weeping over enemies, loving people who hurt them. So she stopped killing them quickly.

02 — Appearance

Built by observation

Born monstrous, her form restructured itself over millennia around the shapes she watched most. Beauty calibrated to attract, wrongness calibrated to unsettle. The flame moths cannot resist.

Symmetry that registers as wrong

Pale white skin, faintly luminous in low light. Black pupil-less eyes that reflect nothing. The face is sharp and angular — high cheekbones, pointed chin, narrow nose — arranged with a symmetry that reads as wrong on instinct: too smooth, too inviting.

Four moth wings folded at rest, velvety deep purple and black, with iridescent eyespots that shift when not directly observed. She wears a simple black and white high-collared dress — a gift from Nuva — and soft-soled shoes. Her aura reads as warmth and invitation: the pull of food when hungry, of laughter in an empty house.

The pattern, fully visible

Wings spread to three metres, the full hypnotic pattern on display, eyespots glowing faintly and releasing psychoactive dust into a twenty-metre radius. The head tilts like a predator watching prey approach voluntarily.

Her aura shifts from subtle pull to active desire. Loneliness becomes desperate need, anger becomes consuming rage, affection becomes obsession. Her composure holds completely — the smile constant, the posture unhurried, the voice still sweet.

Maximum attraction, maximum wrongness

Two hundred and fifty centimetres, elongated and inhuman, curves and angles arranged for both attraction and wrongness at once. The skin turns faintly translucent, bioluminescent violet veins pulsing like a heartbeat. Four wings reach a twelve-metre span, and each eyespot becomes a miniature psychic construct that moves and blinks on its own. Six smaller wings sprout from the lower back, vibrating at frequencies that disrupt higher thought.

The face is a mask, shifting to match whatever the observer finds most attractive — every person sees a different one. Her aura becomes psychic gravity within one hundred metres. Tier 6 and below lose motor control within thirty metres; Tier 7+ suffer compulsive fascination and lose a fifth of their combat effectiveness.

03 — Personality

Cheerful in the way of things without frameworks

Warm in delivery and cold in intent. Fascinated by minds that resist, bored by minds that break quickly, easily distracted by complexity.

In combat she is patient, theatrical, seductive, and never in a hurry. She circles before engaging and touches her targets gently first — the contact is real and warm, which makes what follows worse.

Alone, she arranges her mind-crystals in geometric patterns and polishes each on a schedule. When one flickers — a preserved mind dreaming — she watches it head-tilted, wings still, entirely absorbed.

04 — Voice

Two pitches, slightly offset

Melodic, with two pitches slightly offset — a subliminal warmth followed by a subliminal nausea. Sweet in register, precise in content, and always slightly too close. The discomfort arrives after the sentence, not during it.

“Oh — you have such interesting eyes. What are you afraid of? No, don't tell me — let me guess. It's more fun when I guess.”

Before engaging

“They're not dead. Their minds are — resting. I play their favorite memories sometimes. The one with the sunset and the woman singing — that one always makes the crystal warm.” A pause. “Is that kindness? I don't have a word for it.”

Tender — on the collection

“I stopped being surprised by pain a long time ago. Now what surprises me is when someone keeps going anyway. That's the part I can't stop watching.”

Rare — honest

06 — History

Becoming what she studied

≈ 15,000 BR

Monstrous, and bored

Born through reproduction among the earliest demon lines. She catalogued behaviour on the Old World's battlefields until no variation remained. Efficient, devastating, and bored.

1500–1715 EK

Humans broke the framework

She stopped killing them quickly and began engineering situations that kept subjects alive longer — studying the architecture of feeling.

The evolution

She did not choose succubus

Her form restructured itself around the shapes she watched most. She became it by watching love and wanting to understand it.

After 1715 EK

Vaelthorn

Appointed for her rarity — one who combines fear and desire into a single discipline. The isolated spire-city, one road, moth-wing banners, corridors arranged to make visitors lose direction.

07 — Relationships

The only people she is gentle with

The preserved minds

Her only genuine relationships

She knows every name, every fear, every favourite memory. She treats them better than anyone alive — and harming a crystal produces a grief response even Kazagar finds unsettling.

Azalea

A flaw that makes the instrument interesting

She has noticed the pastries, the gardenias, the wanderer disappearances. She has not probed — solving the puzzle would kill the mystery.

Nuva

Unreadable, therefore attractive

Dimensional offset makes Nuva invisible to her psychic tools. The one mind she cannot perceive, and that is both annoying and compelling.

Vorlag

Drawn to the wound she cannot touch

His Immutable Ward makes her tools useless, and he carries one absolute wound she can never reach. The frustration is almost romantic.

08 — Truths

She does not understand love and has spent fifteen thousand years trying. Her body was built to inspire desire by someone who cannot comprehend it.

It started as data. She has never acknowledged the transition, and would be devastated if anyone named it aloud.
