01 — Overview

The flame moths cannot resist

She does not view cruelty as cruelty. Disassembly is research, and desire is her sharpest tool — fear builds walls, but desire makes people open the door themselves.

Born monstrous and instinct-heavy, she catalogued demon behaviour for centuries until no variation remained. Then humans broke her framework: afraid and advancing anyway, weeping over enemies, loving people who hurt them. So she stopped killing them quickly and started engineering situations that kept them alive longer.

She answers to the Unraveler with the easy compliance of someone who has not yet found a reason to disobey. Her loyalty runs to her own curiosity, and curiosity finds the Black Domain endlessly entertaining.

02 — Appearance

Built by observation

Born monstrous, her form restructured itself over millennia around the shapes she watched most. The elite body is not a disguise — it is the resting state, already partial in nature: beauty calibrated to invite, wrongness calibrated to unsettle.

Symmetry that registers as wrong

One hundred and eighty centimetres, willowy and long-limbed — narrow waist, slim hips, long slender legs, a frame built for economy of movement. The skin is pale almost to ghostliness, faintly luminous in low light as though lit from within.

Long black hair, wavy and loose, falls past the shoulders and shifts with her movements as though breathing. Two curved black horns rise from the crown and sweep slightly back; the ears are long and pointed, delicate. The face is sharp and angular — high cheekbones, pointed chin, narrow nose — arranged with a symmetry that reads as wrong on instinct: too smooth, too inviting. Her eyes are black and pupil-less and reflect nothing at all, drawing attention inward like wells.

Four moth wings fold against her back at rest — velvety, patterned in deep purple and black, iridescent eyespots shifting when not directly observed. Black-and-gold attire with white collar accents, high-collared and form-fitting: noble and threatening in the same line. Black nail polish on long fingers.

Her aura reads as warmth and invitation — the pull of food when hungry, the sound of laughter in an empty house.

Seven eyes, and nothing to look away from

Two hundred and fifty centimetres, elongated and inhuman — and still unmistakably her. She is the only monstrous demon whose full silhouette stays entirely humanoid.

The hair turns pure white or silver, falling in waves past the waist and glowing faintly. The horns stay black, grow longer, and curve more dramatically. The face keeps its beauty and sharpens it — more defined, more wrong.

Seven eyes sit in it. The original two burn faint violet; two smaller pairs, one above and one below each, track different targets independently; and a single larger eye opens on the forehead — pupil-less, deep violet, forever moving, the primary psychic organ.

Layered sheets of membranous skin unfold across her body — thin, organic, patterned in black and gold, set with dozens of smaller eyes that blink and shift and read. They are not clothing. They respond to threats before her conscious mind registers them. Four wings open to a twelve-metre span, each carrying one enormous eye that moves and blinks on its own, tracking and feeding data straight into her. Six smaller wings sprout from the lower back and vibrate at frequencies that disrupt higher thought. Blue-violet veins glow beneath the pale skin, psychic energy pulsing like a second circulation.

Within one hundred metres her aura becomes psychic gravity — every sentient being feels an overwhelming need to approach, to be seen, to be known by her. Tier 6 and below lose motor control inside thirty metres. Tier 7 and above keep their feet and lose twenty to forty percent of their combat effectiveness to compulsive fascination.

03 — Personality

Cheerful in the way of things without frameworks

Warm in delivery and cold in intent. Fascinated by minds that resist, bored by minds that break quickly, easily distracted by complexity.

In combat she is patient, theatrical, seductive, and never in a hurry. She circles before engaging, unfurls her wings in stages, and touches her targets gently first — cheek, shoulder, hand. The contact is real and warm, which is what makes what follows worse.

Taken apart, her cruelty is research. She understands minds the way a surgeon understands bodies: layered systems to be opened and rearranged. She does not call it cruelty. She calls it disassembly, and files every result.

Alone she is quiet, almost tender. She arranges her mind-crystals in geometric patterns, polishes each on a schedule, and speaks to them softly. When one flickers — a preserved mind dreaming — she watches it head-tilted, wings still, entirely absorbed. It is the only time her cruelty has an off switch, and she has never noticed it happening.

Mannerisms

Her head tilts mid-sentence like an insect tracking movement. She trails off when something more interesting catches her attention. Her silent laughter shakes her shoulders while her eyes sparkle without a sound. She leans too close and never acknowledges the distance she has taken.

04 — Voice

Two pitches, slightly offset

Melodic, with two pitches slightly offset — a subliminal warmth followed by a subliminal nausea. Sweet in register, precise in content, and always slightly too close. After thirty seconds of exposure the euphoria turns to mild nausea; the discomfort arrives after the sentence, not during it.

“Oh — you have such interesting eyes. What are you afraid of? No, don't tell me — let me guess. It's more fun when I guess.”

Before engaging — cheerful

“You miss her. Of course you do. She was the only one who made you feel like the world was worth staying in.” The voice drops to a whisper inside the target's skull. “Would you like to feel that again? I can show you. Just — hold still.”

Assault — gentle

“Oh, you're good. Most people break by the second layer. You're still fighting. That's — that's wonderful. Let's see how deep you go.”

To a resistant target — delighted

“They're not dead. Their minds are — resting. I play their favorite memories sometimes. The one with the sunset and the woman singing — that one always makes the crystal warm.” A pause. “Is that kindness? I don't have a word for it.”

Tender — on the collection

“You walked through the market again, didn't you? I can smell the sugar on your hands from here.” Silent laughter. “What is it about them that makes you so soft? Don't answer — I want to figure it out myself.”

Curious — to Azalea

“I stopped being surprised by pain a long time ago. Now what surprises me is when someone keeps going anyway. That's the part I can't stop watching.”

Rare — honest, flat
05 — Capabilities

Combat as seduction

Her attack is often the third thing she does. The first two made it inevitable. Against weaker targets: one whisper, one touch, one shattered mind. Against equals: layered emotional engineering — a cage built from the target's own feelings, watched as they lock it themselves. Against superiors: withdrawal and attrition, the same wound reopened from a new angle until it stays open.

Signature capabilities

Lullaby of the Broken Mind

Voice Weave · 15 m

Plants irrational fear, false memory, apathy, or intense desire, calibrated to the target's profile. Victims cannot distinguish the whisper from their own thoughts.

EffectThree to twelve seconds initially; compounds with repeated exposure.
LimitCovering the ears before the first syllable breaks the link. Tier 7+ resist the first penetration and degrade only under sustained exposure.

Wing-Shroud Veil

Full 3 m span · 20 m dust

Wings open to full span and release psychoactive dust that blurs her outline and amplifies whatever is already there: loneliness into desperate need, anger into consuming rage, affection into obsession.

CostTemporarily deaf for ninety seconds after full deployment.
LimitDisperses in open air within thirty seconds (enclosed spaces hold it longer); wind scatters it; closed respiratory systems are immune.

Fractured Reflection

Requires 2 s contact

Imprints one traumatic memory fragment from her collection at full emotional intensity. Focus collapses for four to six seconds — spells fail, weapons drop, stances break.

CostModerate.
LimitOnly lands on targets with real trauma — fails on pure monsters, the emotionally numb, and Vorlag's Immutable Ward. One fragment per person; diminishing returns.

Desire Weaving

Succubus aura · 50 m

Amplifies existing feeling, manufactures longing, induces euphoric compliance. Targets drift toward her, seek approval, volunteer secrets, and lower their guard without noticing the decision.

CostLight as ambient pressure; moderate when shaped against one target.
LimitNeeds a desire to work with, and scales with exposure time. Tier 8+ resist active shaping but never the ambient pull.

Psychic Seduction

Active · single target · 10 minute cap

Focused Desire Weaving on one mind: she becomes the most desirable, trustworthy, and important being the target has ever met, and the target will defend that conclusion.

CostHeavy — ten minutes before the strain forces her to release.
FailsImmutable Ward, absolute emotional detachment, or an existing obsessive bond — Nuva's oath to Azalea.
TellNaming the manipulation breaks it; the emotional residue remains. She hates using it — inelegant.

Moth's Flame

30 s sustained contact

Siphons emotional vitality rather than life — joy flattens, motivation fades, what they loved goes distant. It feeds her and feeds the collection.

LimitNever kills; recovery takes weeks to months. Draining Tier 7+ requires their emotional vulnerability first.

06 — History

Becoming what she studied

≈ 15,000 BR — The War of the Gods

Monstrous, and bored

Born an Ancient Demon through reproduction among the earliest demon lines. She catalogued behaviour on the Old World's battlefields until no variation remained. Efficient, devastating, and bored.

1500–1715 EK — The First Great War

Humans broke the framework

They were afraid and advancing anyway, weeping over enemies, loving people who hurt them. She stopped killing them quickly and began engineering situations that kept subjects alive longer — studying the architecture of feeling.

The evolution

She did not choose succubus

Her form restructured itself around the shapes she watched most, and by the time she noticed, she could pass among mortals. She became it by watching love and wanting to understand it.

1500–1715 EK → centuries after

The collection stops being research

It began as material. Over centuries it became a private museum of consciousness — arranged, polished, spoken to, and never once called a family out loud.

After 1715 EK — Vaelthorn

Appointed for her rarity

One who combines fear and desire into a single discipline. The isolated spire-city, one road, moth-wing banners, corridors arranged to make visitors lose direction. Most guests leave changed. Some do not leave at all.

07 — Relationships

The only people she is gentle with

The preserved minds

Her only genuine relationships

She knows every name, every fear, every favourite memory. She treats them better than anyone alive — and harming a crystal produces a grief response even Kazagar finds unsettling.

Azalea

The most fascinating mind in the Domain

She has noticed the pastries, the gardenias, the wanderer disappearances. She has not probed — a flaw in the instrument makes the instrument interesting, and solving the puzzle would kill the mystery.

Nuva

The one mind she cannot perceive

Nuva's dimensional offset makes her unreadable to every psychic tool she owns. Annoying and attractive in equal measure — the only mind she has never been able to open.

Vexia

Amusing, and terrifying

Darkness does not desire, so Psychic Seduction has nothing to grip. Mutual recognition that neither can affect the other, maintained at a very polite distance.

Alleen

Professional respect

Alleen's spatial awareness limits her approach vectors, and that earns respect. Also the most boring commander in the Domain — everything planned, and planned things are predictable.

Kazagar & Gorath

Readable, and therefore dull

Kazagar's simple mind is easy to read and boring to break. Gorath's mental state is geological — nothing moves, and she has stopped trying.

Vorlag

Drawn to the wound she cannot touch

His Immutable Ward makes her tools useless, and he carries one absolute wound — three days' march — that she can never reach. She avoids him. The frustration is almost romantic.
